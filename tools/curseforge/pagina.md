## Descrição

**Azimute** is a free leveling and quest guide for **WoW: Forever**, made in Portuguese (pt-BR) with full English support.

### Features
- **Guide window** with the current step, progress bar and compact tips. Right-click a line to mark it as done.
- **Arrow and map pin** to the next objective (optional TomTom support), plus **routes**: flight paths, boats, zeppelins and hearthstone when they are faster.
- **Back to your corpse**: when you die, the arrow leads you to your corpse.
- **Auto accept / turn in** the guide's quests (optional, SHIFT cancels), quest item button, gear suggestions by spec and trainer reminders.
- **Guide picker** that recommends a guide by race, class, faction, level and the quests already in your log.
- **Dungeon quests** panel and guides with dungeon routes.
- **Community guides**: route recorder, editor, import/export.
- Everything automatic can be turned off. No data leaves the game.

### Bundled modules (independent addons — disable any of them without affecting the guide)
- **Azimute Meter** (`/azm`): group damage, DPS, healing, interrupts, deaths and more, using the game's own combat data. During combat the game hides exact numbers from addons, so percentages and spell details appear after the fight.
- **Azimute Bags** (`/azb`): all bags and the bank in one window, with search, sort, free slots, gold and item level.

### Guide coverage (beta)
- Levels 1-30, Alliance and Horde, per race: adapted from the RestedXP guides.
- Levels 30-60: **experimental** guides made for Classic, not yet checked in Forever.

### Found a problem?
Type `/azimute report` (or click the bug icon in the guide window), copy the text and open an issue on GitHub: https://github.com/Jeeeff/Azimute/issues
